PhonePe can use different sign-in protection methods: an authenticator app, approval in the official app, SMS, passkeys, or account confirmation. Add the account to TOTP only when PhonePe shows a QR code or manual key for an authenticator app.

### 1. Open PhonePe

Go to the app or website and sign in.

### 2. Open account settings

Open profile, account settings, privacy, or security settings.

### 3. Find sign-in protection

Look for two-factor authentication, login verification, two-step verification, or additional sign-in methods.

### 4. Choose authenticator app

Select the authenticator app option if it is available.

### 5. Scan or enter the key

Scan the QR code with TOTP or enter the manual setup key.

### 6. Confirm the code

Enter the 6-digit code from TOTP back in PhonePe.

### 7. Save recovery codes

If PhonePe offers recovery codes, keep them somewhere safe. If only SMS, push approval, passkeys, or an official app are available, use that method separately.
