RMIT University supports time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) with an authenticator app. Use this guide only when RMIT University shows a QR code or secret key for an authenticator app. SMS, push, passkeys and hardware keys are configured separately.

Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-and-facilities/it-services-for-students/cyber-safety/multi-factor-authentication

### 1. Open RMIT University

Sign in to RMIT University on the official website or app.

### 2. Open account security

Go to account, profile, sign-in, privacy or security settings.

### 3. Choose two-factor authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication and choose the authenticator app option.

### 4. Scan the QR code

Scan the QR code with TOTP or enter the secret key manually.

### 5. Confirm setup

Enter the 6-digit code from TOTP back into RMIT University.

### 6. Save recovery codes

Store backup codes or recovery methods somewhere safe.
